CONFIDENCE — How cleanly a repeating block edge was found. Under 15% there may be no grid to find — the image may already be 1:1.
The two axes are measured independently, so they can disagree — and unequal cells make non-square art pixels, which quietly changes the aspect ratio of the result. Locked, both take the mean. Unlock only for art that really was stretched on one axis.

A grid can be genuinely ambiguous: art drawn at 16px where column pairs happen to match is also a valid 32px reading. These are the runners-up, most likely first.
OR JUST SET THE ART SIZE
For art that was drawn at full resolution in a pixel style rather than upscaled from a small grid, there is no grid to find — you pick the resolution. This resamples to whatever size you name, with the same cell sampling and palette rebuild.
03 SAMPLING
CELL COLOUR
DOMINANT takes the most common colour in a cell and ignores everything else — the right answer when cells are genuinely flat, i.e. when the grid above locked on. MEDIAN is the safe middle. MEAN averages the cell.

Pick by what the GRID panel said: a recovered grid means flat blocks, so use DOMINANT. A chosen art size means you are downscaling shaded artwork, and DOMINANT will chase the noise inside each cell — MEDIAN or MEAN hold gradients together far better.
How much of each cell's border to ignore. The outer edge is where the anti-aliasing lives; sampling it is what smears two blocks into one.
04 PALETTE
Clustered in OKLab and weighted by how much of the art uses each colour, then snapped to colours that really occur — so no entry is an average sitting between two real ones.
