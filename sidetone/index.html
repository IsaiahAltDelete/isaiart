Reading the numbers

Under about 10 ms of total lag, your monitor blends with the voice you hear through your own skull. That's the target for good sidetone.

Around 20 to 50 ms it stops sounding like you and starts sounding like a slapback echo.

Around 150 to 250 ms is the zone of delayed auditory feedback, where most people start tripping over their words. Try reading a ticket note out loud on the Tongue twister preset.

Est. total adds the browser's audio engine, output and mic figures to your added delay. Bluetooth headsets add a lot the browser can't see, often 100 ms or more, which is why wired or USB headsets sound so much better for this.
