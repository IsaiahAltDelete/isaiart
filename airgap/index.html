Channel settings

The stream is fountain-coded: any frame the camera catches helps, so dropped frames only add time, never stall the transfer. RGB turbo packs three codes into the colour channels of every frame for 3× throughput — needs a decent camera and clean lighting.

How to use

  1. On the offline machine: pick a file, hit Play.
  2. On the other machine: open this same page, go to Receive, start the camera.
  3. Point the camera at the screen. Fill the frame with the QR code.
  4. Leave it running until progress hits 100%, then download.
