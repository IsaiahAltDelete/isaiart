Who opens

Connect Four is a first-player win with perfect play, so who opens matters more than it looks.

Scores reset on reload.

RULES

Take turns dropping a disc into one of the seven columns. It falls to the lowest open slot.

  • Four in a row wins: across, up and down, or diagonal.
  • A full board with no four is a draw.
  • Against the computer you are always red.
  • On Brutal there are no takebacks.

Tip: the middle column touches the most fours. Threats on odd rows favour whoever opened; even rows favour the other side.
