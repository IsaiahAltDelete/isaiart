# Built-in iPhone icon artwork

The 16 Home Screen and Dock icons use unaltered PNG artwork published in Apple's current iPhone User Guide, retrieved October 14, 2026. The same images appear in the app switcher, notifications, and matching Settings > Apps rows. They are local files; the emulator does not depend on Apple image servers at runtime.

`sources.json` records the original page and image URL, dimensions, retrieval date, and SHA-256 digest for every image. These are Apple's published 60 × 60 guide assets, not invented or AI-generated logos. Their resolution is suitable for the current approximately 62-pixel tiles, but does not provide native Retina sharpness at enlarged display scales. Only the standard appearance is represented; alternate tinted/clear/dark icon variants and the live Clock face are not implemented.

The images remain Apple artwork. Source references document provenance; they are not a claim of ownership or affiliation.
