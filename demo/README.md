## Gator's Dockside enrollment demo

An online open enrollment form. Employees choose their benefits, sign, and get a PDF copy. Each enrollment is saved to a private Google Sheet and Drive folder, and Key HR reviews submissions from a password-protected admin page. Key HR staff can also sign in and complete an enrollment on an employee's behalf.

- **Enrollment form:** https://keyhrsales.github.io/demo/gatorsdockside/
- **Key HR admin:** https://keyhrsales.github.io/demo/gatorsdockside/admin/
- **Key HR staff (enroll for an employee):** https://keyhrsales.github.io/demo/gatorsdockside/staff/
- **Slideshow:** https://keyhrsales.github.io/demo/gatorsdockside/slideshow/
- **Reference sheet:** https://keyhrsales.github.io/demo/gatorsdockside/reference/

The old links (`/demo/admin/`, `/demo/staff/`, `/demo/slideshow/`, `/demo/reference/`, `/demo/#staff`) redirect to the new locations.
