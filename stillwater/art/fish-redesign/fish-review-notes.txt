Stillwater fish redesign — 20 October 2026

Six fish rebuilt as continuous procedural meshes with toon materials, restrained
outlines, embedded eyes, species-specific silhouettes, fins, tails and expressions.
Pout: sleepy eyes, downturned mouth and rounded fan tail.
Molar Perch: narrow tall body, striped flank, spiny dorsal, curved gums/tooth plates.
Widow's Wig: elongated body, pointed tail and tapered underside barbels.
Peeping Tom: narrow tall disc body with seven embedded eyes.
Soggy Harold: broad low head, hooded eyes, barbels and broad fins.
The Below: dark body, mouth cup, irregular teeth, heavier lower lip and luminous lure.
