Confirmed fixes:
Below's jaw is continuous with the head; the earlier attached gasket is gone. The isolated inner-mouth oval has become a coherent recessed color region. Pectoral roots and curved membrane rays are cleaner. Reed Crown no longer has crossing crest rays. Bell Carp, Candlefin and Tom now have actually visible bounded markings. Paper Skate has a continuous diamond body and readable planar folds instead of nested contour outlines. Desktop and supplied mobile guide subjects are unclipped and recognizable.

Remaining visible finish issues:
1. Candlefin's cream stripe has a conspicuously serrated/stair-stepped edge in the quarter image, inconsistent with Bell Carp's clean stripe. The surface pattern still looks generated by mesh sampling at this boundary.
2. Widow's underside appendages remain a row of similarly rooted, similarly shaped strands. The coarse squared bases are visible in quarter view. This is the weakest organic attachment design in the set.
3. A number of faces remain schematic: Molar Perch uses uniform rectangular tooth plates in a ring, and several species share the same small ring mouth and circular eye construction. Species differences are now readable, but these repeated detail constructions limit the sense of individually authored creatures.
4. Some broad belly transitions, especially Tom and Candlefin in front view, form irregular patches that do not clearly read as intentional markings or controlled toon planes. The finish is less deliberate than the new flank bands and Paper Skate folds.

Strengths: coherent restrained palette and line language; strong roster silhouette range; successful Pout and Harold expressions; distinctive Below and Paper Skate; substantial improvement over the original primitive-based assets. The main jaw and fin repair goals are achieved in the inspected stills. The overall roster is improved but does not reach the stipulated 8.5 art bar.

Unassessed: continuous motion quality, actual catch presentation, hidden underside/rear surfaces, the other ten mobile guide specimens, physical-device performance. Numerical geometry/data checks and scenery contribute no visual-quality points. All three reviews in this cycle are used; no additional attempt is authorized by this verdict.
