            READ ME
            

                
Threads has, in practice, no search operators at all. It takes one term and gives you Top or Recent. There is no phrase matching, no exclusion, no OR, no author filter, no date range, no media filter and no engagement filter — the entire vocabulary the other two terminals are built around is missing.

                
Everything the native side can address is here: the keyword search (serp_type=default), the topic-tag stream (serp_type=tags), the Recent ordering, and profiles. That is the whole surface.

                
The date-range and from-profile filters Threads added live inside the app's own filter sheet and are not addressable from a URL, so they cannot be pre-built here. For operators, switch to WEB INDEX — a search engine's copy of Threads answers questions Threads itself won't.

            READ ME
            

                
This mode searches a search engine's index of Threads, not Threads. That buys real operators — phrases, exclusions, OR, author scoping, date ranges — at a real cost: you only see what has been crawled. Coverage skews old and public, misses most replies, and lags hours to days behind a live post.

                
Use NATIVE for what is being said right now. Use WEB INDEX for find the post I know exists.

                
inurl: is honoured by Google and Bing; DuckDuckGo supports it inconsistently. before: and after: are Google-only and are dropped from the other two.
